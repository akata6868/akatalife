# Akata Life

**Private Household, Contract, Finance, Document & Home Management Platform**

Akata Life is a self-hosted, production-oriented personal administration system designed for desktop and iPhone PWA usage, localized in **German (`de-DE`, default)** and **Turkish (`tr-TR`)**.

## Core Modules
- **Dashboard:** Real-time monthly income, expenses, remaining budget, expiring contracts, upcoming bills, recent documents, and reminders.
- **Verträge (Contracts):** Track household agreements, automatic notice/cancellation deadlines, renewal dates, and linked documents.
- **Dokumente (Private Document Library):** Secure upload outside the web root, magic-byte validation, authorized streaming preview/download.
- **Finanzen / Einnahmen / Ausgaben:** Exact minor-unit (`EUR` cents) accounting, custom categories, monthly budgets, and recurring schedules.
- **Rechnungen (Invoices & Bills):** Planned, unpaid, paid, overdue, and cancelled invoice tracking with monthly calendar and list views.
- **Termine & Erinnerungen (Reminders):** Prioritized reminders for contract deadlines, bills, warranties, and personal dates.
- **Mein Zuhause (My Home):** Property details, rooms, household appliances, warranty tracking, maintenance logs, and meter readings.
- **Geräte & Energie (Home Assistant):** Optional read-only server-side integration for indoor climate and energy monitoring.
